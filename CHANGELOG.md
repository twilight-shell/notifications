# Changelog

Versions follow [Semantic Versioning](https://semver.org). Until 1.0, a minor version

## 0.1.0 - 2026-10-24

The first release.

- The notification daemon (`org.freedesktop.Notifications`) and its cards: actions, urgency, a fuse that burns down while a card is shown.
- Cards make way for a popout that hangs over their column.
